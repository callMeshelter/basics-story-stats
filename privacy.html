Privacy policy: Basics Story stats

Last updated: 6 November 2026.

What the app accesses

With the permission of the signed-in user, the app reads, in read-only mode, the list of that user's YouTube videos and their statistics from the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API (scopes youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly).

How the data is used

The data is used only to produce statistics reports for the channel owner. It is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Basics Story, on their own account. It is not offered to the public.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account security page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app also complies with the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Basics Story YouTube channel.
